About me

I am an AI Systems Developer focused on designing and building production-grade AI systems using Large Language Models (LLMs). My work centers on translating AI research into scalable, real-world applications across both product and service-based environments. I specialize in developing end-to-end AI systems powered by Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), agentic workflows, and Small Language Model (SLM) fine-tuning. My experience includes building document intelligence pipelines, semantic search systems, and LLM-powered applications that emphasize reliability, contextual accuracy, and practical deployment. At Tata Consultancy Services, I work on Generative AI proof-of-concepts and reusable AI frameworks, including document ingestion pipelines, vector database management, prompt orchestration, and agent-based workflows using LangChain and LangGraph. I focus on system design, experimentation, evaluation, and delivery—owning the full lifecycle from problem understanding to deployable solutions.

From building data pipelines and ML models to crafting RESTful APIs and responsive web interfaces — I enjoy the full spectrum of software development.
